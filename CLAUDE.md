# CareAid — Project Brief

## 1. What we're building

CareAid is a caregiver-first app that reduces the invisible admin burden of caring.

Caregivers do not struggle because they cannot complete individual tasks. They struggle because important information is scattered across conversations, notes, calendars and family messages.

CareAid allows a caregiver to simply describe what happened in their own words. It identifies what matters and helps organise it into:
- updates for family
- reminders and tasks
- appointment questions
- important care events

**One conversation. Organised care.**

The app is not designed to replace the caregiver's judgement. It helps them stay organised while keeping them in control.

---

## 2. The problem

Millions of people provide unpaid care for family members, often while balancing work and their own lives.

The difficulty is not only providing care — it is managing everything around it:

- remembering medication changes
- preparing for appointments
- updating family members
- tracking important events
- keeping information in one place

Today this information is fragmented across:
- WhatsApp conversations
- phone notes
- calendars
- paper documents
- memory

CareAid exists to reduce this mental load.

## 3. User

Primary user:

**Sarah, 52**
- Daughter caring for her mum Margaret
- Works full-time
- Coordinates medication, appointments and family communication

Person receiving care:

**Margaret Ellis, 78**
- Parkinson's
- Lives independently
- Supported by Sarah and paid care

Family member:

**Tom**
- Sarah's brother
- Receives updates

---

## 4. Core experience

The user journey:

1. Sarah opens CareAid
2. She says what happened naturally

Example:

"Mum had a bad night. She missed her evening Sinemet and I found it still in the tray. Dr Okafor changed her dose and Tom wants to know how she is."

3. CareAid identifies what matters
4. CareAid proposes helpful actions:
   - update medication record
   - draft a message to Tom
   - prepare appointment questions
   - add important dates

5. Sarah reviews and approves.

CareAid assists. Sarah decides.

---

## 6. Product principles

1. Caregiver first
The app is built around the person managing care, not only the patient.

2. Human approval
CareAid never sends messages, changes records or creates actions without caregiver approval.

3. No medical advice
CareAid records information and organises tasks. It does not diagnose, recommend treatments or make clinical decisions.

4. Never lose information
The original caregiver input is always preserved and traceable.

5. Simple under pressure
The user may be tired, stressed or busy. The experience should require minimal effort.
